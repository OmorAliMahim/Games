📌 Game Rules

Your goal is to guess a random number between **1 and 100**.
1. The computer will randomly choose a number between **1 and 100**.
2. Enter your guess in the input box.
3. Click the **Guess** button or press **Enter** to submit your guess.
4. You have a total of **10 attempts**.
5. Each incorrect guess will reduce your remaining attempts by **1**.
6. If your guess is **lower** than the hidden number, you will see **"Too low!"**
7. If your guess is **higher** than the hidden number, you will see **"Too high!"**
8. If you guess the correct number, **you win the game!**
9. If you use all 10 attempts without guessing correctly, **Game Over**.
10. After the game is over, the correct number will be revealed.
### 🏆 Your Goal
Try to guess the correct number using **as few attempts as possible!
**Good luck and have fun! 🎮**
